**1. Introduction**

This Privacy Policy explains how REAP Holdings (Pvt) Ltd. (“REAP Holdings”, “the Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), a company incorporated in Sri Lanka with its registered office at \[REGISTERED ADDRESS\], collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data of individuals who visit or use www.en4tainment.com and any related subdomains (together, the “Website”) in the Company's capacity as the operator of the En4tainment talent-booking marketplace.

The Website is the public-facing side of the En4tainment platform, used by Clients (individuals or organisations seeking to book performers) and Venues (businesses hosting live events) to discover, request quotes from, and book Talent. A separate, mobile-focused application, En410, is provided for Talent (performers) and is governed by its own Privacy Policy.

This Policy is intended to comply with the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka (“PDPA”) and other applicable data protection law. By accessing or using the Website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

**2. Definitions**

- Personal Data — any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined under the PDPA.

- Special Category Data — under the PDPA, personal data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, genetic data, biometric data used to uniquely identify a person, health data, data concerning sex life or sexual orientation, data relating to criminal offences or convictions, or any personal data relating to a child (a person under 16).

- Processing — any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, and deletion.

- Data Subject — the individual to whom Personal Data relates (e.g. a Client or Venue representative using the Website).

- Data Controller — REAP Holdings (Pvt) Ltd., which determines the purposes and means of the Processing described in this Policy.

- Talent — performers, artists, and entertainers who create profiles and are bookable through the platform.

- Sub-processor — a third-party service provider engaged by the Company to Process Personal Data on its behalf (e.g. Supabase, Cloudinary).

**3. Personal Data We Collect**

**3.1 Information You Provide Directly**

- Account information: name, email address, mobile number, password (stored as a salted hash), and role (Client or Venue).

- Profile information: for Venues — venue name, registered business name, Business Registration (BR) number, BR certificate copy, registered and physical addresses, contact person details, and Google Maps pin.

- Booking and quote information: event dates, times, locations, budgets, special requirements, and messages exchanged with Talent or Venues through the in-app messaging feature.

- Payment information: cardholder name, card brand, and the last four digits of payment cards, processed via Payable.lk. We do not store full card numbers or CVV codes on our servers.

- Reviews and ratings: star ratings and written feedback submitted about Talent following a completed booking.

- Correspondence: information you provide when contacting us for support, including the content of your enquiry.

**3.2 Information Collected Automatically**

- Usage data: pages visited, features used, search queries, referring URLs, and timestamps.

- Device and technical data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, and device identifiers.

- Cookies and similar technologies, as described in our separate Cookie Policy.

**3.3 Information from Third Parties**

- If you choose to sign in using Google OAuth, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google, and (where you grant the relevant scope) limited Google Calendar access used solely to create or remove calendar events tied to confirmed bookings.

- Payment confirmation data from Payable.lk once a transaction is processed.

**4. How We Use Personal Data**

1.  To create and administer your Client or Venue account.

2.  To operate the core marketplace functions: matching quote requests with Talent, facilitating quotations, confirming bookings, and generating contracts.

3.  To process payments, deposits, and refunds through our payment processor, Payable.lk, and to maintain financial records required for accounting and tax compliance.

4.  To enable communication between Clients, Venues, and Talent regarding a specific booking.

5.  To send booking confirmations, status updates, and service-related notifications via in-app notification, email, SMS, or push notification, according to your notification preferences.

6.  To maintain the star-rating and review system, which is displayed publicly on Talent profiles.

7.  To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms and Conditions.

8.  To comply with applicable legal, tax, and regulatory obligations in Sri Lanka.

9.  To improve, secure, and develop the Website and related services, including through aggregated and anonymised analytics.

10. To feature select Talent profiles or events (“Rising Stars” and “Live & Loud” sections) on the homepage, where such features relate to public Talent profile data rather than Client or Venue personal data.

**5. Legal Bases for Processing**

Under Schedule I of the PDPA, we rely on the following legal bases, depending on context:

- Performance of a contract — to provide the marketplace services you request, including account creation, bookings, and payments.

- Consent — for optional features such as Google Calendar sync, marketing communications, and non-essential cookies. Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

- Legitimate interests — for fraud prevention, platform security, service improvement, and dispute resolution, balanced against your rights and interests.

- Legal obligation — for tax record-keeping, financial reporting, and responding to lawful requests from Sri Lankan authorities.

**6. How We Share Personal Data**

We do not sell Personal Data. We disclose Personal Data only in the following circumstances:

**6.1 With Other Users of the Platform**

- When you submit a quote request or booking, relevant details (name, event details, budget range) are shared with the Talent you are engaging with, and vice versa.

- Reviews and star ratings you submit about a Talent are displayed publicly on that Talent's profile.

**6.2 With Service Providers (Sub-processors)**

We engage the following categories of Sub-processors to operate the Website. Each is bound by contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations:

- Supabase — database hosting, authentication, and backend infrastructure (PostgreSQL, storage, edge functions).

- Cloudinary — storage and delivery of media assets such as profile photos, cover images, and portfolio content.

- Payable.lk — payment processing for bookings, deposits, and refunds.

- Railway — hosting of our webhook and backend notification services.

- Google — where you opt in to Google Sign-In or Google Calendar integration.

- Cloudflare — content delivery and website hosting infrastructure.

**6.3 Legal and Business Transfers**

- Where required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or lawful request by a Sri Lankan regulatory or law-enforcement authority.

- In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets, subject to confidentiality safeguards.

**8. Data Retention**

Consistent with Section 9 of the PDPA, we retain Personal Data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, including:

- Account data: for the duration of your account's existence, and for a reasonable period thereafter to handle disputes or legal claims.

- Booking, payment, and financial records: for the period required under Sri Lankan tax and accounting law (generally not less than five years).

- Messages between users: retained for the duration of the relevant booking relationship plus a reasonable retention window for dispute resolution.

- Audit logs: retained as an immutable record for security and compliance purposes.

Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, Personal Data is securely deleted or anonymised. Certain records — in particular financial, payout, and tax-related records — may be subject to a legal-hold retention obligation under Sri Lankan law notwithstanding an erasure request; where this applies, we will inform you of the specific legal basis for retaining that data.

**9. Data Security**

We implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest where supported by our infrastructure providers.

- Row-Level Security (RLS) policies at the database level restricting each user's access strictly to their own records or records they are authorised to view.

- Role-based access controls distinguishing Client, Venue, Talent, and Administrator permissions.

- Secrets and credentials such as payment gateway tokens and webhook secrets are stored in dedicated secret-management systems, not in application code.

- Sensitive identity-verification (KYC) documents submitted by Talent are stored under authenticated (non-public) access modes.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your Personal Data, we will notify the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka and, where required, notify you, in accordance with Section 23 of the PDPA and any rules made thereunder.

**10. Your Rights**

Subject to the PDPA and its exemptions, you have the right to:

- Access the Personal Data we hold about you, and receive the information set out in Schedule V of the PDPA (Section 13).

- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data (Section 15).

- Request erasure of your Personal Data, subject to our legal retention obligations (Section 16).

- Object to or restrict certain Processing, including direct marketing.

- Withdraw consent at any time where Processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of Processing carried out before withdrawal (Section 14).

- Request a copy of your Personal Data in a portable format, where technically feasible.

- Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 15. We will acknowledge and respond to your written request within twenty-one (21) working days, as required by Section 17 of the PDPA, informing you whether your request has been granted, refused (with reasons, where disclosure is not otherwise prohibited by law), or that we have refrained from further processing pending review. We may need to verify your identity before actioning a request.

**11. Automated Decision-Making**

Where REAP Holdings uses ranking, curation, or recommendation logic to feature Talent profiles in sections such as “Rising Stars”, this relates to Talent (not Client or Venue) profile data, and is based on factors such as rating, completeness, and engagement, combined with administrator curation. This does not involve automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on Clients or Venues.

**12. Cookies**

The Website uses cookies and similar technologies. Full details of the categories of cookies used, their purposes, and how to manage your preferences are set out in our separate Cookie Policy, which forms part of this Privacy Policy by reference.

**13. Children's Privacy**

The Website is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect Personal Data from, individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected Personal Data from a minor without appropriate consent, we will take steps to verify age and delete such data promptly.

**14. Changes to This Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will post the updated Policy on the Website with a revised “Last Updated” date. Material changes will be communicated via email or a prominent notice on the Website prior to taking effect.
