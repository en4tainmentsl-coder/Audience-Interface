**1. Introduction**

This Privacy Policy explains how REAP Holdings (Pvt) Ltd. (“REAP Holdings”, “the Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), a company incorporated in Sri Lanka with its registered office at \[REGISTERED ADDRESS\], collects, uses, discloses, and protects the personal data of Talent (performers, artists, bands, and ensembles) who register for and use the En410 application (“En410”, “App”).

En410 is the Talent-facing side of the En4tainment marketplace. A separate, web-based platform (www.en4tainment.com) is used by Clients and Venues to discover and book Talent, and is governed by its own Privacy Policy.

This Policy is intended to comply with the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka (“PDPA”) and other applicable data protection law. By registering for or using En410, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

**3. Personal Data We Collect**

**3.1 Account & Profile Information**

- Account information: name, email address, mobile number, password (stored as a salted hash), and role.

- Public profile information: stage name, bio, pricing, genres, availability, and uploaded media (photographs, videos, audio).

- Payout information: verified bank account details used to process payouts.

**3.2 Identity Verification (KYC) Data**

To enable payouts, Talent must complete identity verification by submitting a valid National Identity Card or equivalent government-issued identification. This KYC documentation is stored under authenticated (non-public) access, requiring signed delivery URLs to view.

**3.3 Booking, Quote & Communication Data**

- Quote and booking records: quoted amounts, travel/equipment fees, terms, event dates, and booking status.

- Messages exchanged with Clients or Venues through the in-app messaging feature.

- Contracts generated for confirmed bookings, including their content and signature status.

**3.4 Reviews & Ratings**

Star ratings and written reviews submitted about you by Clients following a completed booking, including sub-ratings for stage presence, musical ability, professionalism, sound quality, and audience response.

**3.5 Information Collected Automatically**

- Usage data: features used, search queries, timestamps, and app interaction logs.

- Device and technical data: IP address, device identifiers, operating system, and app version.

**3.6 Information from Third Parties**

Where you sign in using Google OAuth, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google. Payout confirmation and transaction data is received from our payment partner, Payable.lk.

**4. How We Use Personal Data**

1.  To create, verify, and administer your Talent account, including KYC verification.

2.  To operate the core marketplace functions: displaying your public profile, matching you with Quote Requests, processing Quotes, confirming Bookings, and generating Contracts.

3.  To process payouts through Payable.lk and to maintain financial records required for accounting and tax compliance.

4.  To enable communication between you, Clients, and Venues regarding a specific Booking.

5.  To send booking confirmations, status updates, and service-related notifications via in-app notification, email, SMS, or push notification.

6.  To maintain the star-rating and review system displayed publicly on your Talent profile.

7.  To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms and Conditions.

8.  To comply with applicable legal, tax, and regulatory obligations in Sri Lanka.

9.  To feature select Talent profiles in curated sections such as “Rising Stars” or “Live & Loud” on the En4tainment website, based on rating, completeness, engagement, and administrator curation.

10. To improve, secure, and develop En410 and related services, including through aggregated and anonymised analytics.

**5. Legal Bases for Processing**

Under Schedule I of the PDPA, we rely on the following legal bases, depending on context:

- Performance of a contract — to provide the marketplace services you request, including profile hosting, bookings, contracts, and payouts.

- Consent — for optional features such as Google Sign-In, and for any non-essential communications. Consent may be withdrawn at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

- Legitimate interests — for fraud prevention, platform security, service improvement, curated profile features, and dispute resolution, balanced against your rights and interests.

- Legal obligation — for tax record-keeping, financial reporting, and responding to lawful requests from Sri Lankan authorities.

Where processing of your KYC documentation is necessary to enable payouts under your Talent contract with us, this is processed on the basis of contractual necessity (Schedule I(b)).

**6. How We Share Personal Data**

**6.1 With Other Users of the Platform**

- Your public profile (stage name, bio, media, pricing, genres, availability, ratings) is visible to Clients and Venues browsing the En4tainment website.

- When you submit a Quote or accept a Booking, relevant contact and event details are shared with the corresponding Client or Venue.

**6.2 With Service Providers (Sub-processors)**

We engage the following categories of Sub-processors to operate En410. Each is bound by contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations:

- Supabase — database hosting, authentication, and backend infrastructure (PostgreSQL, storage, edge functions).

- Cloudinary — storage and delivery of media assets, including authenticated, signed-URL storage of KYC documents.

- Payable.lk — payment processing for payouts.

- Railway — hosting of our webhook and backend notification services.

- Google — where you opt in to Google Sign-In.

- Cloudflare — content delivery and website hosting infrastructure.

**6.3 Legal and Business Transfers**

- Where required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or lawful request by a Sri Lankan regulatory or law-enforcement authority.

- In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets, subject to confidentiality safeguards.

We do not sell Personal Data.

**8. Data Retention**

Consistent with Section 9 of the PDPA, we retain Personal Data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy:

- Account and profile data: for the duration of your account's existence, and for a reasonable period thereafter to handle disputes or legal claims.

- KYC documentation: retained for as long as your account remains active and payout-enabled, and thereafter for the period required to meet financial record-keeping obligations.

- Booking, contract, and payout records: for the period required under Sri Lankan tax and accounting law (generally not less than five years).

- Messages: retained for the duration of the relevant booking relationship plus a reasonable retention window for dispute resolution.

- Reviews: retained permanently as part of the public rating record, consistent with Section 9 of these Terms, save where removed at REAP Holdings' discretion for abuse, fraud, or manifest error.

- Audit logs: retained as an immutable record for security and compliance purposes.

Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, Personal Data is securely deleted or anonymised.

**9. Data Security**

We implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, in line with Section 10 of the PDPA, including:

- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest where supported by our infrastructure providers.

- Row-Level Security (RLS) policies at the database level restricting each user's access strictly to their own records or records they are authorised to view.

- Role-based access controls distinguishing Talent, Client, Venue, and Administrator permissions.

- Authenticated (non-public), signed-URL access for sensitive KYC documents.

- Secrets and credentials such as payment gateway tokens and webhook secrets stored in dedicated secret-management systems, not in application code.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your Personal Data, we will notify the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka and, where required, notify you, in accordance with Section 23 of the PDPA and any rules made thereunder.

**10. Your Rights**

Subject to the PDPA and its exemptions, you have the right to:

- Access the Personal Data we hold about you, and receive the information set out in Schedule V of the PDPA (Section 13).

- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data (Section 15).

- Request erasure of your Personal Data, subject to our legal retention obligations (Section 16).

- Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, or object to processing based on legitimate interests, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal (Section 14).

- Request a copy of your Personal Data in a portable format, where technically feasible.

- Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 14 \[renumber cross-reference to Contact section below\]. We will acknowledge and respond to your written request within twenty-one (21) working days, as required by Section 17 of the PDPA, informing you whether your request has been granted, refused (with reasons, where disclosure is not otherwise prohibited by law), or that we have refrained from further processing pending review. We may need to verify your identity before actioning a request, and may request additional information to do so.

Certain records — in particular financial, payout, and tax-related records — may be subject to a legal-hold retention obligation under Sri Lankan law notwithstanding an erasure request; where this applies, we will inform you of the specific legal basis for retaining that data.

**11. Automated Decision-Making**

Where REAP Holdings uses ranking, curation, or recommendation logic to feature or rank Talent profiles in discovery surfaces (for example, “Rising Stars”), this is based on factors such as rating, profile completeness, and engagement, combined with administrator curation. This does not constitute a decision producing legal or similarly significant effects on you without human involvement, and administrators retain the ability to review and adjust featured placements.

**12. Children's Data**

En410 currently requires all Talent to be at least 18 years of age at the time of registration; no pathway exists for the registration of Talent under 18. Accordingly, En410 does not knowingly collect Personal Data from a “child” as defined by the PDPA (a person under 16). If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected Personal Data from a person under 18 in breach of our eligibility requirements, we will take steps to verify age and, where appropriate, suspend the account and delete the relevant data promptly. Should REAP Holdings introduce a verified minor-Talent pathway in the future, this Policy will be updated to reflect the Schedule II(a) parental/guardian consent mechanism that would apply.

**13. Cookies & Similar Technologies**

En410, as an installable web application (PWA), may use local device storage and similar technologies to maintain your session and preferences. Where applicable cookie-equivalent technologies are used, they are limited to those strictly necessary for the App's core functionality.
