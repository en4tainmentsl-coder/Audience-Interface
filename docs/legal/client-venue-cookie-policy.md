**1. Introduction**

This Cookie Policy explains how REAP Holdings (Pvt) Ltd. (“we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on www.en4tainment.com (the “Website”). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains more broadly how we handle your Personal Data under the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka (“PDPA”).

By continuing to browse or use the Website, and where required by law, by providing your consent through our cookie banner, you agree to the use of cookies as described in this Policy. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via the cookie preference settings described in Section 6.

**2. What Are Cookies?**

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They are widely used to make websites function properly, to remember your preferences, and to provide reporting information. Similar technologies covered by this Policy include local storage, session storage, pixels, and tags.

**3. Categories of Cookies We Use**

|                                       |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Category**                          | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Strictly Necessary Cookies**        | Required for the Website to function — for example, maintaining your logged-in session (Supabase authentication tokens), enabling secure checkout via Payable.lk, and remembering items in a booking request in progress. These cannot be disabled without breaking core functionality. |
| **Functional Cookies**                | Remember your preferences, such as your selected currency or previously searched location, to improve your experience across visits.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Analytics / Performance Cookies**   | Help us understand how visitors interact with the Website (e.g. pages viewed, search terms used, time on page) so that we can improve performance and content. Used only where you have consented.                                                                                      |
| **Third-Party / Integration Cookies** | Set by third-party services embedded in the Website, such as Google (for Google Sign-In and Calendar integration) and our content delivery network. These are governed by the respective third party's own privacy and cookie policies.                                                 |

**4. Specific Cookies and Similar Technologies**

|                                   |                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Cookie / Technology**           | Category \| Duration \| Purpose                                                                                                                                                                  |

Note: the specific third-party analytics or advertising tools in use may change as the platform evolves. Where a new category of non-essential cookie is introduced, we will update this Policy and, where required, request fresh consent.

**5. Why We Do Not Use Advertising Cookies**

REAP Holdings does not currently sell advertising space on the Website, and does not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies to build advertising profiles of visitors. If this changes in the future, this Policy will be updated in advance and your consent will be requested where required by law.

**6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences**

1.  Cookie banner: on your first visit, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner allowing you to accept all, reject non-essential, or customise your preferences by category.

2.  Browser settings: most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings menu. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from logging in or completing bookings on the Website.

3.  Google Account settings: if you have used Google Sign-In, you can review and revoke third-party access at any time via your Google Account security settings.

**7. How This Relates to Our Privacy Policy**

Analytics and functional cookies described in this Policy are processed on the basis of your consent, which you may withdraw at any time as described in Section 6, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Strictly necessary cookies are processed on the basis of contractual necessity, as they are required to deliver the Website's core functionality to you. Where any cookie data is processed by a Sub-processor located outside Sri Lanka (such as Cloudflare or Google), this is covered by the cross-border transfer disclosure in our Privacy Policy.

**8. Changes to This Policy**

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in the cookies and technologies we use. Material changes will be indicated by an updated “Last Updated” date and, where appropriate, a renewed consent prompt.
